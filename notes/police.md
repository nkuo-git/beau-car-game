Rules: punch +1★, hit (car hits person) +2★, cop (hit/punch police car or officer) +1★, shoot = 3★ at once, shootCop +1★, gunfire +1★ only if police within 60 m; same type once per 1.5 s; max 3★. One police car per star (2.5 s stagger), from V.places.police.spawn if within 600 m else on a road 200–400 m away out of view; road network, right lane; roles tail / get ahead / alongside, box in when slow; unstick + relocate (8 s unseen stuck, 20 s max, >520 m behind). Taiwanese two-tone siren (Web Audio), red/blue light bars, minimap dots (grey when going home). Caught in car: <5 km/h with police car within 7 m for 2.5 s, or ≥2 cars within 6.2 m for 1.5 s. On foot: officers get out, run 5 m/s (player 4.5), catch within 1.2 m; return to car if >60 m or you get in your car. Arrest: 「被警察抓到了！」 → fade → cell 30 s or pay fine 2 萬/star (money.spend) → station door, car in yard, stars reset. Escape: unseen 25 s → −1★, then every 10 s; inside a house (hidden) = unseen; own garage door closed (safe) = clear all. At 0★ police go home, vanish out of view. Police never shoot; people fall and get up; no blood; arrest never touches guns/ammo.
